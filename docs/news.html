LATEST RELEASES

Facets 4.0

Facets 4.0 continues the modernization begun in 3.2 for Ruby 3.1 and newer. The 3.2.0 release changed enough that it should have been 4.0.0; 4.0.0 makes the next round of changes under the right number.

4.0.027 SEP 2026

LATEST

Clearer by design

Makes #try strict and #try! lenient, moves Rails-compatible methods to a new lib/rails area, and schedules every deprecation for removal after 2027-09-30.

3.2.215 JUN 2026

Compatibility, polished

Fixes range intersection on Ruby 3.1 and 3.2, restores Range#overlap? where Ruby does not provide it, and corrects Binding#caller_locations argument handling.

Read changes ↗
3.2.114 JUN 2026

Core loading restored

Repairs stale require paths that affected require 'facets' in 3.2.0, and adds a load-path regression check. Also adds OpenDSL and PIC.

3.2.0APR 2026

Modern Ruby foundation

Moves the minimum Ruby version to 3.1, adds methods such as Array#to_ranges and String#dashcase, and retires extensions now built into Ruby.

FROM THE ARCHIVE

Over twenty years
of ideas.

Facets grew out of TomsLib, a handful of useful methods started in July 2002. It was renamed Succ (2003), A.B.C. (2004) and Raspberry (2004) before becoming Ruby Facets in December 2004, with 1.0 following a year later.

These posts are preserved as published. Their install advice and API links describe older Facets releases; use the current guide for 3.x.
